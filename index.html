Album · 12 Sept 2026

SINNER

The fall, finally. After the crown and the halo comes the confession nobody asked for — eleven songs about wanting the wrong things loudly. Recorded over nine nights in a deconsecrated chapel in Deptford, SINNER trades the choir loft for the back seat, pitching gospel harmony against blown-out 808s and red-light synths.
