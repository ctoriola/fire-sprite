Biography

Lord,Saint,Sinner is a South London singer, songwriter and producer making gospel noir — soul music for people who were raised to be good and grew up anyway.

London, UK
Biography

Raised in the
choir loft

Lord,Saint,Sinner grew up between two churches: the Pentecostal hall in Peckham where they sang every Sunday from the age of seven, and the back rooms of South London raves where they learned what a bassline could do to a body.

They started making music alone in a box room in 2019, releasing songs online under three different names — a cold, regal trap alias, a devotional soul singer and a messy, funny confessional songwriter. When people started to notice that the three were the same person, the name stuck: Lord,Saint,Sinner, one word, no spaces.

Signed to FireSprite Ent in 2021, they spent the next five years building a trilogy of records, one for each voice. LORD (2022) was the swagger, SAINT (2024) the devotion and SINNER (2026) the confession. Together they've been streamed more than 600 million times.

“I used to think I had to choose,” they say. “Now I think the whole point is that you don't. You can be all three on the same Tuesday.”

The three

Meet the
masks

Each record belongs to one of them. On stage, you get all three.

Lord

The one who shows up first. Confident to the point of cruelty, dressed in gold, built to survive rooms that didn't want them.

Sounds like: cold synths, trap drums, a Juno-60 on its last legs.

Saint

The one who was raised in the choir loft. Tender, devotional, afraid of how much they want to be good.

Sounds like: stacked harmonies, upright piano, candle hiss.

Sinner

The one who tells the truth. Messy, hungry, funny about it — the voice underneath the other two.

Sounds like: blown-out 808s, red-light synths, a choir that should know better.

Joins the Peckham Mass Choir aged seven. Learns harmony before learning to read music.

2019

Box-room demos

Starts recording alone on a borrowed Juno-60 under three names, then realises they're all the same person.

2021

Signed to FireSprite Ent

A SoundCloud demo of “Throne of Glass” reaches FireSprite Ent's A&R team. A deal is signed in a Nando's.

LORD

The debut EP. “Throne of Glass” is added to national radio playlists within a month.

2024

SAINT

2025

Prize shortlist

SAINT is shortlisted for the Southbank Sound Prize. “Candlelight (Live)” is released.

2026

The trilogy closes. The Trinity Tour begins at O2 Academy Brixton in October.
