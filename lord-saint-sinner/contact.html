Before you write

Good to
know

Can I send a demo or feature request?

We can't accept unsolicited music or feature requests, for legal reasons. If you're an artist, FireSprite Ent's A&R team accepts demos through the label's own site.

Where do I send fan mail?

Letters and drawings can go to: Lord,Saint,Sinner, c/o FireSprite Ent, PO Box 666, London SE15. Every single one gets read.

I have a problem with a ticket.

Tickets are sold by each venue's official partner, so please contact them first. If you're still stuck, email access@lordsaintsinner.com.

Can I use a song in my video?

For personal, non-commercial social posts, go for it — the songs are cleared on the major platforms. For anything commercial, email sync@firesprite.ent.
