The show

Three acts.
One night.

Act I is the crown: cold light, gold and bass. Act II is the halo: the choir walks in, the candles are lit. Act III is the fall — red light, fire and SINNER front to back.

Closing song

Absolution · 5:20

When do tickets go on sale?

Congregation members get presale access 48 hours before general sale. General sale opens at 10am local time on the date listed for each show.

Is there an age limit?

Most shows are 14+ (under-16s must be with an adult). Festival dates follow the festival's own policy. Check the venue listing for specifics.

What's the running order?

Doors open at 7pm, the support act is on at 8pm, and Lord,Saint,Sinner plays at 9pm for about 100 minutes across three acts. Times can vary by venue.

Is the show accessible?

Every venue on the tour has accessible viewing areas and step-free access. Email access@lordsaintsinner.com and we'll arrange companion tickets and anything else you need.

What can I do if a show is sold out?

Join the waitlist on the show row. Returned tickets are released through the official ticket partner at face value — please avoid resale sites.

Can I bring a camera?

Phones are fine. Professional cameras and recording equipment need a press pass — contact press@lordsaintsinner.com.
