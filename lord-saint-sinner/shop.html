When will my order ship?

In-stock items ship within 3 working days. Signed vinyl ships from 26 September. Pre-orders ship on the date shown on the product.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — to over 60 countries. UK delivery is £4.50 (free over £60). EU from £9, US and Canada from £12, rest of world from £15.

What's your returns policy?

Unworn apparel can be returned within 30 days. Signed and limited items are final sale unless they arrive damaged.

How do the sizes fit?

Tees are a relaxed unisex fit; hoodies are oversized. If you're between sizes, size down for a regular fit.
