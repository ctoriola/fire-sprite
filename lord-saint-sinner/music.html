Chapter III
Album · 12 Sept 2026

SINNER

The fall, finally. After the crown and the halo comes the confession nobody asked for — eleven songs about wanting the wrong things loudly. Recorded over nine nights in a deconsecrated chapel in Deptford, SINNER trades the choir loft for the back seat, pitching gospel harmony against blown-out 808s and red-light synths.

Chapter II
Album · 3 May 2024

SAINT

The breakthrough. Ten songs of devotion written after the noise of LORD, when the only thing left to do was get quiet and mean it. Candlelit, choir-stacked and softly strange — SAINT spent 31 weeks on the UK R&B chart and turned “Halo Static” into a late-night anthem.
